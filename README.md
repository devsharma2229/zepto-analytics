# 🛒 Zepto Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Zepto's product inventory and pricing dataset using PostgreSQL and Power BI.

The objective is to uncover business insights related to product pricing, discount strategies, inventory management, category performance, and revenue estimation through SQL analysis and interactive dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and validation.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- Analyze pricing and discount strategies.
- Estimate category-wise revenue.
- Evaluate inventory availability and stock status.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.

---

## 📊 Business Questions Solved

### Data Exploration

- Count total records.
- Identify null values.
- Find unique product categories.
- Analyze stock availability.
- Detect duplicate products.

### Data Cleaning

- Remove products with invalid pricing.
- Convert prices from paise to rupees.

### Business Analysis

#### 1. Top 10 Best Value Products
Identify products offering the highest discount percentages.

#### 2. High MRP Products Out of Stock
Find premium products currently unavailable.

#### 3. Revenue Analysis
Estimate category-wise revenue.

#### 4. Premium Products with Low Discounts
Identify products priced above ₹500 with discounts below 10%.

#### 5. Category Discount Analysis
Determine categories with the highest average discount.

#### 6. Price Per Gram Analysis
Evaluate product value based on weight.

#### 7. Product Segmentation
Classify products into Low, Medium, and Bulk categories.

#### 8. Inventory Weight Analysis
Calculate total inventory weight across categories.
